Turn 2 · Bringing back the hills

Three doses of the original's landscape

All three are 1d with the example pins, definitions off. They differ only in how much of the original peaks-and-beams motif comes back: a full landscape (2a), light beams descending from the band (2b), or a whisper behind everything (2c). The peaks never sit under a label as a container, so they can't be read as buckets.

2a Full landscape Peaks of varying height sit below the band; the tallest three coincide with the reference points.

Turn 1 · Continuum redesign

One continuous spectrum, three reference points

Three treatments of the same structure, each answering your two open questions differently. All are wide web banners (1180 px), all keep every word as live text on its own layer for relocalisation, and all carry a non-colour cue (hairline density) alongside the labels. 1d plots your examples; 1e is the draggable version.

1a Capped ends, ticked continuum Faint full-height ticks orient you; only the two endpoints are walled off.
